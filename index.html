Design & coordination · 2025 — 2026

A trigger on every bet

Lets operators react to each individual bet (for example, a bonus after a big win) instead of waiting for a periodic recalculation.

~2.75 s
from bet to action
3
production stacks
How it works
  • A batch Kafka consumer on game-round events groups bets per casino and matches them against pre-compiled trigger matchers.
  • In-process LRU caches with TTL hold compiled workflows and per-player counters; counters are flushed every 30 s and on eviction, rebalance or shutdown.
  • Cache invalidation is broadcast over Redis pub/sub; guardrails limit trigger count and minimum bet.

Scheduled waits in workflows

“Wait 3 days, then send the offer.” Pauses a workflow for each player and resumes it within a 30-second scheduling window.

How it works
  • Added PostgreSQL to a MongoDB service: weekly range partitions, created ahead and rotated by cron.
  • State machine: waiting → queued → started → finished / skipped / failed.
  • A cron job every 5 minutes picks up overdue delays and pre-enqueues the next 10 minutes via Sidekiq bulk push; a failure monitor watches for stuck delays.
